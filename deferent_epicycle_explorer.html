Deferent & Epicycle

The ancient trick for explaining a planet's backward "retrograde" loop without the Sun at the center: ride a small circle (the epicycle) whose own center travels around a big circle (the deferent).

A retrograde loop appears whenever the epicycle's own edge-speed (Re×ωe) outpaces the deferent's (Rd×ωd) — exactly the geometric trick ancient astronomers used to reproduce a planet's backward motion in the sky.
