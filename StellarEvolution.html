Reading the chart. Left is hot and blue, right is cool and red. Up is bright, down is dim. Most stars spend their lives on the diagonal main sequence.
Dashed boxes hold stages that don’t fit on the chart: some are far too bright (supernovae, for a few weeks), too hot and tiny (neutron stars), too cold (gas clouds), or not shining like a star at all (black holes, gas remnants).
Mass decides the path. Over 8 Suns: supergiant, supernova, neutron star or black hole. Under 8 Suns: giant, planetary nebula, white dwarf. Under 0.08 Suns: never becomes a real star at all.
