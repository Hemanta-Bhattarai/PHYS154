Phases of the Moon

In reality the Moon rotates once per orbit, so the same face always points at Earth — its rotation and orbit are locked together. Here the spin is a free, separate control: watch the cyan surface marker turn away from Earth as you spin the Moon, showing what we'd see if that locking didn't hold. Note both "Play month" and "Spin Moon" advance at the same rate — press Play All to see them stay in sync, just like the real, tidally-locked Moon.

Speeds are calibrated to real time: Earth turns once every 24 hours, and the Moon orbits (and spins) once every 27.3 days. At 1×, that's about 6 seconds per Earth day and ~2.7 minutes per Moon orbit — speed it up to watch more cycles pass.
