Interactive Astronomy Tools

Four standalone HTML explorers covering Earth's tilt, the phases of the Moon, sunrise & sunset across the seasons, and solar/lunar eclipses. Each file is fully self-contained — open it directly in any browser, no server or install required.

Earth-Centered Ecliptic Explorer

A geocentric view: Earth fixed at the center with its equatorial plane held flat, while the ecliptic — the Sun's apparent yearly path — is the plane tilted against it. Drag the pole handle to change the tilt (obliquity) and watch the equinox/solstice markers shift.

Phases of the Moon

Earth and Moon both spin in place — drag the Moon around its orbit and watch the phase disk ("as seen from Earth") update in real time. Includes a local sky/horizon view showing when the Moon rises and sets for each phase, calibrated to real orbital periods.

Moon phasesTidal lockingReal-time calibrated

Sunrise & Sunset

An observer's local horizon, with the Sun's actual daily path plotted for the June solstice, equinox, and December solstice side by side. Drag the celestial pole marker to change latitude and see sunrise/sunset azimuth and day length shift accordingly.

Solar & Lunar Eclipses

Sun-centered, with Earth orbiting once a year and the Moon's tilted orbital plane following Earth around. Shows the line of nodes, eclipse seasons, and both shadow cones, plus live "as seen from Earth" views of the Sun and Moon during an eclipse.
