Earth's Equator & the Ecliptic

Earth stays fixed here, equator held flat. The ecliptic — the Sun's apparent yearly path — is the plane tilted against it. Where the two planes cross gives the equinoxes; where the Sun sits farthest from the equator gives the solstices.
